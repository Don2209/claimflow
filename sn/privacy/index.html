Privacy notice

This is a plain-language draft that has not yet had legal review. If you have a question, write to us at rufaro@visyntech.co.zw.

Who we are

ClaimsFlow is a product of VisynTech (Visyn Technologies), based in Harare, Zimbabwe. For anything about your data, write to rufaro@visyntech.co.zw.

What we collect

The details you send us through the demo and pilot forms: your name, work email, phone number, company, role, country, time zone and message.

Why we use it

To reply to your request and talk with you about a demo or pilot. We do not use these details for anything else.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your details. The services that host this website or deliver your message may process them on our behalf, and those services can be in other countries.

How long we keep it

Only for as long as we need them to deal with your request. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection law may let you see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Write to us and we will help.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics are off. This site remembers your light or dark choice in your own browser, and it sets no tracking cookies.
